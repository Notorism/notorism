# Constructivism

## What this label means

A Russian avant-garde associated with early twentieth-century social transformation. Rodchenko and other practitioners treated art as construction and communication.

Category: Art movements. This entry supplies a visual vocabulary, not permission to change the user’s product or scope.

## Defining mechanics

- Diagonals.
- geometry.
- red blocks.
- forceful type.
- montage.

## Suitable subjects

Poster exhibition, Cultural manifesto, Workshop program are starting contexts for analysis. Choose this language only when the audience, content, and primary task support it. Explain which mechanic belongs to the subject.

## Implementation decisions

Use hierarchy and composition before decoration. Resolve the actual product’s existing tokens first. The study palette is #e9ddc9, #20201f, #be3028, #7e817b. Its roles are canvas, foreground, accent, and supporting surface. Measure actual contrast; the sample palette is not an accessibility certificate.

Keep reading text clear while applying the distinctive treatment to the appropriate visual plane. Match image material and icon weight to the direction. ## History and evidence

The origin paragraph is a concise contextual introduction. Community labels may have disputed boundaries and no known founder. Distinguish a style’s history from the history of a curatorial archive.
