# Product copy

Write for the person using the product. Name actions and objects in their
language. Technical terms belong in a developer tool when those terms help its
users make a decision; they do not belong in unrelated customer-facing flows.

## Sentence rules

Use active, concrete sentences. Avoid em dashes and en dashes as rhetorical
punctuation. Split the sentence or use a comma where appropriate. Ordinary
hyphens in real terms, negative numbers, and literal source data remain valid.

Avoid stock contrast frames such as “It’s not about X, it’s about Y,” “Not X,
but Y,” and “More than X.” State the outcome directly. Avoid colon-and-reveal
slogans, invented dramatic labels, and stock phrases such as “unlock your
potential,” “seamless experience,” “revolutionary,” or “game-changing” unless
precise, substantiated product content actually requires them.

Product specificity matters more than cleverness. Do not invent testimonials,
client logos, ratings, prices, security claims, customer counts, metrics, or
certifications. Demo content must be labeled as demo content. The reference
library’s fictional subjects are studies, not real customer evidence.

## Controls and states

| Weak copy | Useful copy | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get started | Book a visit | Names the action |
| Something went wrong | We couldn’t save your changes. Try again. | Names the failure and recovery |
| Webhook configuration | Delivery notifications | Uses customer vocabulary when that is the actual function |
| It’s not about storage, it’s about freedom | Keep your files available offline. | Describes a concrete benefit |
| Continue | Review your booking | Names the next step |

An action label must match its behavior. Specify cost, permanence, or affected
objects when needed to make the decision. Empty states explain what belongs
there and provide a useful next step. Loading states must not imply success.
An error should preserve entered information when possible.

The CLI provides a heuristic copy scan. Its findings require editorial review;
they are not proof that all writing is useful or that every matched phrase is
wrong in context.
