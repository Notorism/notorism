# Motion and spatial experiences

Motion must explain a state change, guide attention, preserve context, or serve
the requested narrative. Record its trigger, duration, easing, affected element,
and reduced-motion alternative. Hover is not the only trigger on touch devices.

The page must be complete at rest. Essential content cannot wait at opacity zero
for an observer. Do not override ordinary scrolling to force a performance.
Parallax amplitude should be modest. A magnetic visual can move while its hit
area stays stable. A custom cursor cannot replace native keyboard focus or touch
feedback.

For 3D, budget scene complexity and loading weight. Explain what the scene lets
the person do. Provide controls, progress during genuine loading, a meaningful
static alternative, and a way to recover when rendering fails. Do not advertise
WebGL behavior if the delivered demo is a static illustration.

Interaction references include stills for visual analysis and HTML demonstrations.
Some demonstrations approximate a spatial or contextual idea instead of shipping
a complete device runtime. Their metadata must say which. A screenshot cannot
prove motion behavior; inspect the demo and record runtime evidence separately.
