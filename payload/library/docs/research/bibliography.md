# Bibliography

These references support particular decisions. Descriptions are original paraphrases; no book passages or invented page citations are included.

## Steve Krug, Don’t Make Me Think, Revisited (2014)

[Primary source](https://sensible.com/dont-make-me-think/). Scanning, familiar conventions, and usability testing. Original synthesis, not a transcription of the book.

## Don Norman, The Design of Everyday Things, revised edition (2013)

[Primary source](https://jnd.org/books/the-design-of-everyday-things-revised-and-expanded-edition/). Signifiers, feedback, constraints, and conceptual models. Interaction foundations, not a style catalog.

## Robert C. Martin, Clean Code (2008)

[Primary source](https://www.informit.com/store/clean-code-a-handbook-of-agile-software-craftsmanship-9780132350884). Clear names, focused responsibilities, and maintainability. Implementation guidance, not an aesthetic rule or a reason to overengineer.

## W3C, WCAG 2.2 Quick Reference

[Primary source](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/). Contrast, keyboard access, focus, text alternatives, and interaction targets.

## Google, Material Design

[Primary source](https://m3.material.io/). Components, semantic states, color roles, and motion.

## Google, Material Design: Elevation

[Primary source](https://m2.material.io/design/environment/elevation.html). Depth as a hierarchy of surfaces.

## Apple, Liquid Glass introduction, June 9, 2025

[Primary source](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/06/apple-introduces-a-delightful-and-elegant-new-software-design/). Origin of the named Liquid Glass language. A CSS approximation does not reproduce the complete native optical material.

## Nielsen Norman Group, Skeuomorphism

[Primary source](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/skeuomorphism/). Physical metaphors, perceived affordances, and recurring material treatments.

## MoMA, Bauhaus and Beyond

[Primary source](https://www.moma.org/calendar/galleries/5388). Context for the school founded by Walter Gropius in 1919.

## MoMA, Constructivism

[Primary source](https://www.moma.org/collection/terms/constructivism). Russian avant-garde and its social context.

## MoMA, De Stijl, 1917–1928

[Primary source](https://assets.moma.org/documents/moma_catalogue_1798_300159061.pdf). Movement history and original documents.

## MoMA, Art Nouveau

[Primary source](https://www.moma.org/collection/terms/art-nouveau-new-art). Organic lines, applied arts, and architecture.

## Design Museum, Memphis

[Primary source](https://designmuseum.org/memphis). The Italian group and its public debut in 1981.

## Tate, Art Terms

[Primary source](https://www.tate.org.uk/art/art-terms). Movement vocabulary. Consult the specific entry before assigning dates or authors.

## Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute, Aesthetics

[Primary source](https://cari.institute/aesthetics). Curated archive of consumer aesthetics. Retrospective labels are not necessarily formally founded movements.

## CARI, Project History

[Primary source](https://cari.institute/history). History of cataloging Y2K and other aesthetics. Archive founders are not necessarily creators of the aesthetics.

## CERN, The birth of the Web

[Primary source](https://home.cern/science/computing/birth-web). Web history, distinct from a universal visual definition of Web 1.0.

## MDN, CSS reference

[Primary source](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference). Current implementation of surfaces and effects. Not evidence of historical authorship.

## MDN, CSS Grid Layout

[Primary source](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/CSS_grid_layout). Grids, DOM order, and responsive composition.

## MDN, prefers-reduced-motion

[Primary source](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-motion). Functional alternatives when people reduce animation.

## MDN, CSS scroll-driven animations

[Primary source](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/CSS_scroll-driven_animations). Scroll timelines and progressive enhancement.

## MDN, Variable fonts guide

[Primary source](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/CSS_fonts/Variable_fonts_guide). Actual variable font axes and how to use them.

## MDN, WebGL API

[Primary source](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGL_API). Browser 3D rendering, limits, and alternatives.

## W3C, SVG 2

[Primary source](https://www.w3.org/TR/SVG2/). Vector geometry, filters, and graphic semantics.

## Ink, official v7.0.0 documentation

[Primary source](https://github.com/vadimdemedes/ink/tree/v7.0.0). React terminal rendering and keyboard interaction.

Books guide usability, interaction, and implementation. They are not evidence that a visual style has a particular founder. Check editions and exact page numbers against the actual book before making direct citations.
