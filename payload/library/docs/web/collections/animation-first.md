# Animation-first sites

Sites where the motion is the work: WebGL scenes, scroll choreography, kinetic type, custom cursors. Reference class: Igloo, Lusion, Cuberto, Active Theory.

101 sites. ★ marks hand-picked anchors; the others were measured and qualified.

**What to look for:** Study how the scene or the scroll tells the story, how the motion is timed and eased, the loader and entry sequence, the performance and reduced-motion handling, and how legibility survives the effects.

Open the live site, observe, then design something original. Never reuse a site's markup, copy, imagery, logo or brand.
